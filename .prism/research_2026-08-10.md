## Validation search (industry practice + agentic EDA research)

- **Feature selection (MI / L1 / RFE) is still current interview material**:
  dataquest.io's 2026 data-analyst interview guide and multiple 2026
  anomaly-detection interview-question compilations both name mutual
  information, RFE, and PCA-based selection as expected knowledge, and
  anomaly-detection questions explicitly probe "how do you decide on the
  algorithm" and "how do you handle false positives" — i.e. explainability
  of the flags, not just the flags themselves.
  ([dataquest.io](https://www.dataquest.io/blog/data-analyst-interview-questions-and-answers/),
  [Devinterview.io anomaly detection Qs](https://github.com/Devinterview-io/anomaly-detection-interview-questions))
- **LLM-assisted feature selection is an active research direction**, not
  just a Prism idea — 2025/2026 papers on LLM-guided semantic feature
  selection explicitly benchmark against MI/RFE/Lasso as the baseline set
  a serious tool is expected to offer before any LLM layer sits on top.
  ([ScienceDirect, LLM-guided semantic feature selection](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2773186325001471))
- **Agentic AI adoption context**: Gartner's cited 40%-of-enterprise-apps
  embedding agents by end of 2026 (up from <5% in 2025) is the backdrop for
  why this cycle's priority theme (auto-EDA, anomaly narration, proactive
  insights) is the right thing to keep investing in, not just this run's
  arbitrary pick. ([MachineLearningMastery, 7 Agentic AI Trends 2026](https://machinelearningmastery.com/7-agentic-ai-trends-to-watch-in-2026/))
